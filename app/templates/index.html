How it works

  1. Intake – paste notes, LinkedIn text, or old resume content. AI structures a master career database (no invention).
  2. Add jobs – paste a job description or URL. The agent scores fit on skills, level, domain, location, legitimacy.
  3. Tailor – one click generates ATS single-column PDF + DOCX + cover letter using only your real experience.
  4. Interview – STAR story bank + negotiation scripts from your real experience.
  5. Track – move jobs through Saved → Scored → Tailored → Applied → Interview → Offer.
